Gaze Go: pay in a mart with your iris

Featured FYP

Gaze Go – Iris Payment App

A payment app for shopping marts. Customers don’t need cash, a card or a phone at checkout. They just look into the scanner and pay.

  • Nothing to carry. No cash, ATM card or mobile needed.
  • Set up from home. Create your account in the app before you go.
  • Built for marts. The system is installed at the mart’s checkout.
  1. Sign up at home. Create your account in the app.
  2. Visit a partner mart. The scanner is already installed.
  3. Look into the scanner. Your iris confirms who you are.
  4. Payment is done. The amount leaves your account and reaches the shopkeeper.
